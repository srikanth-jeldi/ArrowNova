Help for ArrowNova by EpitomeHub Technologies.

Contact us

Email contactus@epitomehub.com with the subject “ArrowNova Support”. Include your phone model, Android version, app version and a short description of the issue. A screenshot can help; do not send passwords or private account details.

How to play

Tap an arrow whose exit direction is clear. Remove the blocking arrows first, then clear the whole shape. A blocked tap costs one heart. After three mistakes, restart the puzzle. Use pinch-to-zoom for precise taps on dense boards. Two practice rounds explain movement and blockers before the Classic journey.

Hints and ads

Tap Hint and choose to watch a rewarded ad. A safe arrow is highlighted after the ad network confirms the reward. Ad length varies; skipping before earning the reward does not grant a hint. If an ad is unavailable, continue playing and try again later. Practice hints are free. Banner ads and occasional interstitials support the game; interstitial opportunities occur at a natural break after three new Classic level completions, when an ad is ready.

Daily challenge and garden rewards

Open Daily challenge from Home for the day's puzzle. The first completion earns 40 coins and 10 garden points. Replaying can improve your stars but does not repeat the reward or advance Classic levels. Your daily streak and stars are saved on this device. Garden decorations use earned points and may also require Classic milestones.

Daily reminders

In Settings, enable Daily reminders and allow Android notifications. Reminders are scheduled around 9 AM, 1 PM and 7 PM in your phone's local time. Completing today's challenge stops the remaining challenge reminders for that day when scheduling succeeds. Turn them off in Settings at any time. If notifications are missing, check Android notification permissions and battery restrictions; Android may delay delivery.

Progress and offline play

Classic puzzles and daily boards are playable offline. Ads require internet and available inventory. Progress is stored locally, with no ArrowNova account or cloud restore. Clearing storage, reinstalling or changing phones may lose progress. Please contact support before clearing storage when troubleshooting.

Sound, vibration and privacy

Sound and vibration can be changed in Settings. Available ad consent controls appear under Privacy choices. Read our privacy policy at https://www.epitomehub.com/arrownova-privacy.
