ArrowNova: Arrow Puzzle

Short description:
Clear sculpted arrow puzzles, protect your hearts and grow a crystal garden.

Full description:
Think. Move. Solve.

ArrowNova is a single-player arrow puzzle game with sapphire-blue paths, gentle sound effects and a crystal-garden world. Tap an arrow with a clear exit, remove blockers in the right order and clear the whole shape.

800 CLASSIC PUZZLES
Start with 30 arrows and work towards intricate boards with up to 200. Short and long paths form circles, diamonds, shields and leaves. Pinch to zoom when you need a closer look.

LEARN BY PLAYING
Two guided practice rounds demonstrate arrow movement and blocked paths. In regular puzzles, each blocked tap costs one heart. Three mistakes mean a fresh attempt at the same puzzle.

STARS AND GARDEN REWARDS
Clear a puzzle with fewer mistakes to earn more stars. Unlock decorations with progress and earned garden points, then arrange your garden your way. Replays can improve your best star rating without repeating first-win rewards.

A DAILY CHALLENGE
Return for the day's puzzle, build a local streak and earn a first-completion reward. Optional reminders can be enabled in Settings for around 9 AM, 1 PM and 7 PM in your phone's local time. You can turn them off at any time.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
Classic and daily puzzles work offline. No account is required. Sound and vibration controls let you adjust the experience.

ArrowNova contains ads. An optional rewarded ad can highlight a safe arrow when a hint is needed; ads require an internet connection and availability. Progress is stored on your device and is not synced through an account.
